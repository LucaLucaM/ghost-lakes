# World scan review (by eye), 2023 -> 2026

26,336 urban cells of 0.1 degrees were scanned. 503 have any water -> land change (1,712 ha in total). 105 have 3 ha or more, and 86 of those are radar-confirmed (cells.csv).
48 of the largest cells that are not river channels were checked against Sentinel-2 images (2023 H1 vs 2026 Mar-Nov). Each cell is labelled below by its main cause.

| Label | Cells |
|---|---|
| Official port / coastal reclamation (public, already known) | Lagos coast (Eko Atlantic), Hai Phong, Laem Chabang, Damietta port, Semarang sea wall, Dubai, Luanda bay, Incheon, Valencia port, Palm Jebel Ali, Johor, Annaba port, Kota Kinabalu, Singapore, Zhanjiang, Ruwais, Penang, Sanya, Darwin, Dalian, Jeddah, Rizhao, Tema port, Weihai, IJmeer (IJburg), Pointe-Noire port, Durres, Yokkaichi |
| River channel / sandbar / bank change (natural or river works) | Krishna delta, Hyderabad (Indus), Godavari (Telangana), Teresina, Da Nang, Hangzhou (Qiantang), Phnom Penh riverbank, plus most Ganges / Brahmaputra / Mahanadi / Irrawaddy cells |
| Ponds, pits or quarry lakes filled | Miami-Dade quarry lake, Ghent settling basin, Samut Sakhon ponds, Suzhou, Jackson MS, Machala |
| Salt / evaporation ponds drying | Khuzestan (Iran) |
| Lake works (dikes / islands) | Lake Manzala (Egypt) |
| **Lagoon infill inside a city's neighbourhoods** | **Abidjan (5.25 N, -3.95 E: 20.9 ha, radar-confirmed)**, Lagos lagoon (6.45 N, 3.55 E: small) |

Conclusion: most urban water -> land change on Earth in 2023-2026 is large official port or coastal reclamation. Abidjan is found by the blind global scan without being told where to look. It is one of only two cities where the change is lagoon water being filled inside neighbourhoods.
Limitation: the scan only covers open water that was stable for decades (JRC >= 50%). It misses vegetated marsh, and it misses fills smaller than about 1 ha per cell.
